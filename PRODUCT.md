# PRODUCT.md — Madrasatul Quran, Dhaka

## What it is
Bilingual (Bengali default / English) website for Madrasatul Quran, an Islamic day school in Uttara, Dhaka (House 37, Road 2/A, Block-i, Sector-17; 600 m east of Uttara Center metro). Content is fully CMS-driven (Sanity).

## Unique mechanism
The **50-50 model**: Islamic and general (NCTB) education carry equal weight in curriculum, syllabus and routine — inside one 10-year integrated plan, so a child finishes Dakhil able to go on to either madrasa or college. Hifz is done in a day-care format (7:00 am–8:30 pm) without dropping general study. Arabic and Islamic studies (Nursery–class 8) authored by AQVI (Al Quraner Bhasha Institute), aligned to the Madrasa Board Mutawassita syllabus; Islamic studies taught **in Arabic from class 6**. 300+ hadith, 90%+ Quran translation.

## Stages (owner-approved names)
Pre-Hifz (Nursery–1, 9:00–1:00; Quran 25 / Arabic 25 / General 50) · Hifz day-care (2–5, 7:00–8:30 pm; 70/15/15) · After-Hifz (6–8, 8:00–1:00; Arabic+Islamic 50 / General 50) · Dakhil (9–10) · Mutawassita (class 11, one in-house year after Dakhil).

## Audience
Practising, educated, urban Dhaka parents (engineers, doctors, professionals) choosing between "Islamic but academically weak" and "academic but secular" schools. Many are skeptical of madrasa quality. They decide over weeks, read on phones, share links in family WhatsApp groups, and want proof: curriculum structure, daily routine, people, safety, fees.

## Success
A parent leaves understanding the 50-50 promise, trusting the people and service (day-care hifz, transport on Uttara & Mirpur routes, safety, parent communication), and takes the next step: visit, call, download prospectus, join admission waitlist (pre-admission form currently closed).

## Brand commitments (owner)
- Brown/sand is the brand; green only for tiny status/success marks. Never green buttons or bands.
- Homepage must lead with curriculum uniqueness + service quality (a generic "photo hero → pillars → news" home was rejected).
- All copy is owner-approved; new copy is drafted as `[DRAFT]` and needs sign-off. No hard-coded content in production.
- Do not publish the "80-20" competitor contrast.
- Bengali-first typography must be first-class, not a fallback; Arabic (Quranic) script appears with reverence.

## Constraints
Next.js 16 App Router, Tailwind v4, Sanity v5, next-intl (locales `bengali`/`english`). Real photography is limited (photo shoot planned). Mobile-first: most visits from Android phones on mid-range networks.
