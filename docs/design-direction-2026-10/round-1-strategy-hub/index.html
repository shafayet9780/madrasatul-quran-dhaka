01

The diagnosis

Most school sites feel dated for the same five reasons: they are organised around the institution's org-chart (About / Message from Chairman / Notice board) instead of the parent's questions; they say everything at the same volume; they use stock photos and carousels; their numbers sit in plain tables; and the decisive facts (fees, hours, transport, what my child will actually learn) are hidden in a PDF. World-class product sites do the opposite. Here are the ten moves we are borrowing, and what each one means for MQD.

Apple — product pages

Scroll-pinned storytelling

One idea per screen, the section pins while the story advances. For MQD: the 10-year journey (Pre-Hifz → Hifz → After-Hifz → Dakhil → Mutawassita) pins on screen while the year counter and stage card advance with scroll.

Stripe

Data made beautiful

Numbers become crafted diagrams, not tables. For MQD: each stage's subject split (25/25/50, 70/15/15, 50/50) is a living balance bar; fees become a clean, comparable card, not a scanned notice.

Linear

Restraint and motion timing

Few colours, one weight of emphasis, motion of 200–400 ms on a single easing curve. For MQD: brown/sand only, one accent, no bouncing icons, nothing moves without a reason.

Aesop

Tone and materiality

Paper-like surfaces, long calm sentences, generous margins. For MQD: sand paper tones, serif Bengali display, the quiet confidence of a place of learning rather than an advert.

Airbnb

Trust architecture

Real faces, verified details, clear rules before you commit. For MQD: named advisors and directors with credentials, the 3-document checklist, a real map, real phone numbers one tap away.

Vercel / Arc

Typography as the hero

Big confident type carries the page when photos are absent. For MQD: a first-class Bengali display face sets "৫০-৫০" at poster scale — the type is the brand until the photo shoot lands.

Notion / Things

Progressive disclosure

Show the headline; the detail is one tap away. For MQD: every stage, fee and FAQ opens in place (accordions, bottom sheets on mobile) — parents never lose their place.

Monocle / FT Weekend

Editorial rhythm

Alternating scale — big statement, quiet paragraph, a striking number. For MQD: the home reads like a feature story about a curriculum, not a list of links.

Spotify Wrapped

Shareable moments

Designed, pre-cropped cards built for messaging apps. For MQD: "50-50 in one picture", "A hifz day 7:00–20:30", "Fees at a glance" — each with a 1080×1350 share card and a link preview made for WhatsApp.

Google Maps / Citymapper

Place made concrete

Distance and route, not just an address. For MQD: "600 m east of Uttara Center metro station; 2.5 km from Mirpur DOHS" drawn as a simple stylised map with the transport routes.

06

Design-system principles

Bengali-first type

  • Bengali is designed first, English adapts. Bengali display: a serif with real character (e.g. Noto Serif Bengali / Tiro Bangla); body: Hind Siliguri or Noto Sans Bengali at ≥17px, line-height 1.7 (conjuncts need air).
  • Bengali numerals in Bengali UI (৫০-৫০, ২০,০০০).
  • Arabic in Amiri, used only for real content; Quranic text only in context, never as ornament or background texture.
  • Subset fonts by locale; max 2 families per page load.

Brown/sand brand rule

  • Brown is the brand: buttons, links, active states, data bars.
  • Sand/paper for surfaces; deep umber for text.
  • Green appears only as tiny status marks (✓ open, success toast). Never a green button or band.
  • Full dark theme derived from the same browns.

Motion principles & budget

  • One easing curve, 200–400 ms UI, 600–900 ms storytelling reveals.
  • Motion explains (balance fills, day clock advances, path splits) — never decorates.
  • CSS + IntersectionObserver / scroll-driven animations; no heavy animation library on mobile. JS for motion ≤ 15 KB.
  • prefers-reduced-motion shows the final state instantly.

Imagery & the photo shoot (#1 upgrade)

Natural light, warm, documentary; hands and books more than posed faces; respect for privacy (no identifiable children without consent).

  • Hifz circle at dawn, 7:00 light
  • Close-up: mushaf, Arabic notebook, NCTB maths workbook side by side (the 50-50 shot)
  • Teacher listening to a recitation
  • Science/maths class in progress
  • Lunch/meal service, clean kitchen
  • Transport: van, driver and attendant at pick-up
  • Building exterior + street from metro direction
  • Classrooms, library, wudu area, prayer space
  • Portraits of advisors, directors, teachers
  • Evening: day-care children going home at 20:30

Iconography

One thin-stroke line set (1.5px), rounded joins, drawn in brown. No clip-art mosques, no stars-and-crescent decoration. Geometric pattern only as a subtle tile at 4–6% opacity, never behind text.

Performance & trust bar

Core Web Vitals (mid-range Android, 4G)

  • LCP ≤ 2.0 s (hero is text, not an image)
  • INP ≤ 150 ms
  • CLS ≤ 0.05 (font metrics fallback for Bengali)
  • JS ≤ 120 KB gzipped per route; images AVIF/WebP via cdn.sanity.io
  • Lighthouse mobile ≥ 90 on every page

Accessibility & SEO

  • WCAG 2.2 AA; brown on sand checked ≥ 4.5:1
  • Tap targets ≥ 44px; full keyboard paths
  • hreflang bn/en pairs on /bengali and /english; localised metadata and OG images per page
  • School + LocalBusiness JSON-LD with address, phones, geo
